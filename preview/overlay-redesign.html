Edge handle: drag vertically to set volume in 10% steps; tap to show or hide the chip.
Movable chip: drag it anywhere on screen by its background.
Bar only: no plus or minus buttons. Tap Media ▾ to switch streams.
Notification: one slim row with a progress strip, no action buttons.

Notes on the Android side

  • The notification bar is display-only: Android RemoteViews cannot deliver a continuous drag gesture. Real dragging happens on the overlay chip and the edge handle.
  • The "Volume Manager is displaying over other apps" notice is generated by Android itself while SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW is in use; the app cannot hide it. Removing it entirely means dropping the floating-over-apps feature (a Quick Settings tile would be the alternative).
  • The chip will be built with plain framework views and guarded WindowManager calls, with a gesture detector so a tap reliably toggles it instead of killing the service.
