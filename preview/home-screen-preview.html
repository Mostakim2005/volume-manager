Identified Traps in Current Version:
  • Silent Volume Jumps: Vertical drag changes volume without any HUD or tooltip showing the new level.
  • Gesture Ambiguity: Dragging vertically along the edge changes volume instead of repositioning the handle.
  • No Dismiss Path: Chip has no close button, no outside-tap dismiss, and never auto-closes.
  • Edge Navigation Clash: Swiping the pill triggers Android's "Back" arrow gesture.
